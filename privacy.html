Privacy Policy

Calendar Reminder

A Windows desktop app that watches Google Calendars you've been given access to and reminds you before events start.

Last updated 2026-09-13 · ← Back to the Calendar Reminder homepage

AccessRead-only Google Calendar data. Never write access.
StorageOn your own PC only — nothing in any cloud database.
SharingNever sent to a third party, ever.
ControlRevoke access in one click, any time.

1. What Google data this app accesses

When you sign in with Google, Calendar Reminder requests exactly one OAuth permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly — read-only access to the calendars your Google account can see, including your own and any shared with you.

It never requests, and cannot obtain, permission to create, edit, or delete your calendars or events, or to access anything else in your Google account — Gmail, Drive, Contacts, or otherwise.

2. What this app does with that data

  • Lists your calendars so you can choose which ones to monitor.
  • Reads upcoming event details — title, start and end time, location, and which calendar it belongs to — for the calendars you selected.
  • Uses that information locally to show an "Upcoming Events" list and to trigger a reminder — a Windows notification and/or an on-screen alert — before or at the start of an event, on the schedule you set.

That is the entire purpose of the data access. Nothing else happens with it.

3. Where your data is stored

Everything lives only on your own computer, under %APPDATA%\CalendarReminder\:

  • Event details and your settings — which calendars you selected, reminder timing, colors — are cached in a local database file so the app can work without calling Google's servers every second.
  • Your Google sign-in token is stored using Windows' own Credential Manager, never written to a plain file anywhere on disk.
  • A local log file records basic operational messages, such as a successful sync, for troubleshooting. It never contains your calendar content or sign-in token.

4. What this app never does

  • Never sends your calendar data anywhere except Google's own API — no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no third-party service of any kind.
  • Never shares, sells, or otherwise discloses your data to any person or company.
  • Never creates, modifies, or deletes your calendars, events, or any other Google account setting.
  • Never uses your data for advertising, or for any purpose beyond showing you the reminder you asked for.

5. Your control over this data

  • Sign out any time from the app's Settings screen, to remove your stored sign-in token from that computer.
  • Revoke access entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions — this cuts the app off from your Google account immediately, from Google's side, regardless of anything happening on your computer.
  • Delete all locally stored data by deleting the %APPDATA%\CalendarReminder\ folder, or by uninstalling the app.

6. Changes to this policy

If what this app accesses or does with your data ever changes, this page will be updated to reflect it before that change ships.
