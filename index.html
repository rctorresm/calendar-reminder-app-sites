Windows Desktop App

Calendar Reminder

Watches the Google Calendars you've been given access to, and won't let a reminder go unnoticed.

What it does

A lot of calendar reminders assume you own the calendar. Calendar Reminder doesn't — it's built for someone who manages or supports other people's schedules and needs a heads-up before their events, on calendars shared with a read-only view. It runs quietly in the system tray, checks the calendars you choose, and fires a Windows notification plus an on-screen alert before each event starts — configurable down to the exact start time.

Multi-calendarMonitor several shared calendars at once, each with its own color.
Configurable lead timeFrom 30 minutes before down to the exact start.
Hard to missA toast notification plus a screen-border flash until acknowledged.
Read-only, alwaysCan't create, edit, or delete anything on your calendars.

How it works

  1. Sign in with the Google account that has view access to the calendars you want reminders for.
  2. Pick which of those calendars to monitor, and give each one a color.
  3. Set how far ahead you want a heads-up — the app checks every 30 seconds and fires right on schedule.
